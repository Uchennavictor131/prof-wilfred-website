ABOUT

About the Lab

Our laboratory covers a wide range of equipment, facilities and services for scientific research, investigation, experimentation, engineering design, development and product testing. Among these include solar energy brooding systems, egg incubators, crop and manure dryers for sustainable poultry production, potable solar water heating system, solar cookers, biomaterials handling, transportation, and storage, cloud computing of things peak, treatment and solar desalination of seawaters, climate and environmental engineering services, Food and bioprocessing of fruits and vegetable examplezobo drink making, production Tiger nut solid milk, designing and fabrication of postharvest structures. We also undertake proximate and nutrients analysis of biomaterials.

SERVICES

Laboratory Services

Our consultancy unit undertakes and gives professional and expert advice to companies, governments, NGOs, on renewable energy-related projects, remote sensing and monitoring, environment, climate, farm mechanization, food engineering and technology.
