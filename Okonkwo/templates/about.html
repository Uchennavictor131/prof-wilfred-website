BIOGRAPHY

About {% if profile.name %} {{ profile.name }} {% else %} Prof. Wilfred I. Okonkwo {% endif %}

Prof. Wilfred Ifeanyi Okonkwo is a certified professional Engineer and a University lecturer. He holds Ph.D, M.Eng and B.Eng. in Agricultural Engineering from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN) and the University of Agriculture, Makurdi, Nigeria.

Prof. Okonkwo previously served as the Head of the Department of Agricultural and Bioresources Engineering, UNN, from 2012 to 2016, where he instructs courses in Food and Bioprocess Engineering, Renewable Energy Technologies and Climate.

He served as a Unit Head, Solar Thermal, at the National Centre for Energy Research and Development (NCERD), Energy Commission of Nigeria (ECN), Nsukka Centre, for several years from 2005 to 2006 and from 2008 to 2012.

In addition to the University of Calabar and Federal University of Wukari, Nigeria, he has served as a visiting Professor at numerous universities, including Rivers State University of Technology, Port Harcourt.

He has completed a number of renewable energy projects and worked as a consultant for USAID and UNIDO on the Cross River State Renewable Energy Master Plan Draft and the well-known Ebonyi State Solar Desalination of Okposi and Uburu lakes water project in Ebonyi State.
